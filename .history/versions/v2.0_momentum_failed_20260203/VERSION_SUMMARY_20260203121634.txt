KEY LEARNINGS:
1. N-bar breakout on 3x leveraged ETFs is fundamentally flawed
2. Buying at N-bar high = buying at worst price, immediate drawdown
3. TQQQ doesn't trend intraday - constant oscillation around VWAP
4. Neither tight (1%) nor wide (5%) stops fix a bad entry signal
